How it helps

Get feedback that points to the next move.

  • Score with context: the number is a starting point, not a fake final answer.
  • Read the problem: tonal balance, stereo, loudness, and dynamics feedback stays in plain English.
  • Use real audio: longer playback gives the plugin more to judge before you trust the score.
  • Upgrade when ready: Lite and Full are designed as separate installs so producers can compare cleanly.
