The unfiltered mix coach

Not a preset pack. Not a magic button. A better way to hear.

Your Mix Sucks is built around professional growth: it points to the issue, explains the reason, and keeps the producer responsible for the creative call. The plugin does not replace taste. It trains it.

Coach

Harsh, but useful.

The voice is direct because vague feedback wastes sessions. If the low end is lying, the plugin says so.

Craft

Every note should teach.

Recommendations are written to help producers understand translation, balance, dynamics, and decision-making in market-ready audio.

How it helps

Get feedback that points to the next move.

  • Score with context: the number is a starting point, not a fake final answer.
  • Read the problem: tonal balance, stereo, loudness, and dynamics feedback stays in plain English.
  • Use real audio: longer playback gives the plugin more to judge before you trust the score.
  • Upgrade when ready: Lite and Full are designed as separate installs so producers can compare cleanly.
