Automatic trigger plan

Fast enough to sell, controlled enough for beta.

Wave 1 requests go into the Fix Your Sound Notion queue first. Approved testers receive a tracked access code and the right Lite or upgrade path after review.

01Submit request

The tester sends product interest, Mac details, macOS, DAW, consent, and email through the secure Notion intake.

02Check the cap

Wave 1 stays capped at 250 approved testers before new requests move to the next wave.

03Approve access

Approved testers get a tracked code like FYS-W1-0001 for support and traction reporting.

04Offer upgrade

Lite users see exactly what Full and Suite unlock when they are ready to pay.

Upgrade path

No plugin conflict, no mystery after purchase.

01Try Lite

Lite builds use separate plugin names, so a producer can test without replacing a paid build later.

02Feel the ceiling

Lite proves the problem and shows what the full product unlocks next.

03Upgrade full

Full YMS, full BassPhat, and the Suite are positioned as the paid path after Lite earns trust.

04Keep both available

Lite and full installs should coexist during comparison, then support can guide cleanup if the user wants only full versions.
