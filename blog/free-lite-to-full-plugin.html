Lite to Full

Free Lite first, upgrade only if it helps.

The cleanest plugin offer is not complicated. Let producers try Lite first. If it helps their real sessions, the full version earns the upgrade.

That is better than pretending every producer should buy on faith.

Fix Your Sound can make the first step simple: Lite gives a useful taste of the diagnosis experience, Full adds more depth, and Suite makes sense when the producer wants the full product family.

Why Lite matters

Producers are skeptical for good reasons. They have downloaded enough plugins that looked cool and did nothing meaningful. They have watched enough demos where the song was already mixed.

So Lite should answer one question during beta:

Does this help me hear one real problem faster?

If yes, the upgrade has a reason. If no, we learn from the beta.

What Lite should not be

Lite should not feel broken. It should not feel like a fake button with a paywall attached. It should be limited, but still useful.

The product principle is simple: give enough value to be useful, and save enough depth for the paid version to matter.

The user angle

Lite should reduce risk for the producer. If the plugin helps one real decision faster, the upgrade has a reason. If it does not, the producer learned that without being forced into a purchase first.

That is a healthier relationship than asking people to trust marketing screenshots before they hear the tool in their own session.
