Schools + studios

The bulk-license play starts with classrooms and rooms.

Fix Your Sound is not only a one-producer plugin. The bigger question is what happens when the same mix diagnosis language lives across a classroom, lab, studio, or producer program.

That is the school and studio thesis.

Not as a claimed revenue line yet. As a practical B2B test.

Why schools make sense

Music students need feedback. Teachers need repeatable language. Labs need tools that students can actually understand.

A plugin that helps explain likely low-end, harshness, headroom, and balance problems in plain English could become more than a plugin. It could become a teaching surface.

The value is not just the button. The value is shared vocabulary: mud, harshness, headroom, stereo image, low-end control, vocal balance, and pre-bounce checks.

If a whole class is using the same language, feedback gets faster.

Why studios make sense

Studios have a different problem. They need sessions to move. They need assistants, engineers, producers, and clients to speak clearly about what is wrong.

If Fix Your Sound can help a room catch obvious issues earlier, a bulk license could make sense. But that has to be tested.

The first question is not how many seats can we sell. The first question is whether this makes the room move faster.

What we can say now

The honest product line is simple: schools and studios need clearer mix feedback inside the tools producers already use.

That is credible. That is testable. That does not pretend contracts already exist.
