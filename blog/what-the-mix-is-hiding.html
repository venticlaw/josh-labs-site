Positioning

A plugin that tells producers what the mix is hiding.

Most producers do not need another plugin that looks expensive. They need a plugin that tells the truth before the bounce.

That is the simple idea behind Fix Your Sound.

The vocal is sharp. The 808 is loud but not big. The low end is fighting the kick. The stereo image feels wide until the important part disappears. The master is being pushed harder than the song can handle.

The real pain is not ignorance

Most producers already know when something feels wrong. They play it in the room, then the headphones, then the car, then the phone.

The issue is not that producers cannot hear. The issue is that the problem is hard to name while you are still creating.

Name the problem before the bounce.

Why this matters now

Music is not slowing down. IFPI reported that global recorded music revenues reached US$31.7 billion in 2025, with streaming still driving the market. Luminate's 2025 year-end report points to a crowded release environment where a huge volume of tracks is reaching platforms.

That does not mean every producer needs to become a mastering engineer. It means more people are making release decisions faster, often without a second set of trusted ears.

The product promise

Fix Your Sound is not trying to make every producer sound the same. The product promise is simpler: help producers hear what the mix is hiding before they bounce, upload, send, or sell the record.

That is the why. That is the core functionality. Everything else is execution.
