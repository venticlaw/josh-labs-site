Workflow

Before you bounce, ask better questions.

The bounce button feels like progress. Sometimes it is just a screenshot of problems you did not want to name.

Fix Your Sound should own the moment right before export. Not after the song is already out. Before the damage travels.

The handwritten checklist

  • Does the vocal hurt when the hook gets loud?
  • Is the 808 big, or only loud?
  • Is the kick still punching?
  • Are the drums exciting or brittle?
  • Is the low-mid area cloudy?
  • Does stereo width make the important parts weaker?
  • Is there enough headroom, or is the master fighting back?

Why meters alone are not enough

Meters matter. But peak level alone does not explain perceived loudness, translation, harshness, or the way a listener experiences the track.

That is why loudness standards and playback normalization exist. EBU focuses on loudness measurement instead of peak-only decisions, while Spotify and Apple both describe playback loudness features that can affect how music is heard by listeners.

Where Fix Your Sound fits

Open the plugin. Listen. Get the short diagnosis. Decide what to fix. Then bounce with more confidence.

Not because the plugin made the record perfect. Because the producer stopped guessing in the dark.
