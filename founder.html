The point

Plugins should help producers get better, not just add another window.

Music is more accessible than ever. That is a good thing. More people can record, produce, and release without waiting for a gatekeeper to say yes.

But access also means more people are making decisions alone: low end that does not translate, harshness that sneaks in late, loudness that feels bigger in the room than it does in the real world, and mixes that sound close but still miss.

Fix Your Sound was built for those producers. People like Josh: music-first, technical enough to care about what is really happening, and impatient with tools that look impressive but do not make the next decision clearer.

Music first

The tool should serve the record.

The goal is not to impress producers with complexity. The goal is to help them hear what needs attention and get back to making music.

Plain English

Feedback should make the next move obvious.

Your Mix Sucks is blunt because vague feedback wastes time. A score only matters when it points to a fix.

Real sessions

Low end has to survive outside the room.

BassPhat exists because bass is not just volume. It is weight, color, focus, and translation across real playback systems.

Founder note

Fix Your Sound is for producers who care enough to get better and honest enough to let the plugin call out the mix before the audience does.
